Legend

  • Open surface. Flat, drive freely.
  • Small object. Below the step limit. Drivable at a cost.
  • Obstacle. Too tall or too steep.
  • Keep-out margin. Within one rover radius of an obstacle or edge.
  • Off surface. No scan data, or past the table edge.
How it works
  1. RANSAC finds the table plane in the point cloud and levels it.
  2. Points are binned into a 1 cm height grid; a median filter removes LiDAR spikes.
  3. Each cell is classified by step height, rover tilt across its footprint, and roughness. Obstacles and edges are inflated by the rover radius.
  4. A* finds the cheapest safe route on the cost grid.
  5. The grid becomes a MuJoCo heightfield. A 4-wheel skid-steer rover follows the route with pure pursuit, under contact physics.
Scanning your own table
  1. Use Scaniverse, Polycam or 3D Scanner App in LiDAR mode, 30–50 cm above the table.
  2. Cover the whole tabletop slowly, including the edges.
  3. Export a point cloud as PLY and upload it here.
